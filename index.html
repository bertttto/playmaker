Playmaker — Sandbox Mode

Playmaker is a digital soccer tactics board and strategy training tool built to make soccer IQ more visible and more trainable. It is meant for coaches, players, and teams to map out spacing, build-up patterns, passing options, defensive shape, movement ideas, and game plans in an interactive way. This sandbox is the coaching foundation: drag players onto the field, place them precisely inside each square, attach or free-place the ball with fine control, draw arrows to teach runs and patterns, and quickly switch between common 7v7, 9v9, and 11v11 formation presets.
